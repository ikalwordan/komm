BYTE FRENZY / NATIVE C

You are KERNEL, a small fish in a data ocean. Eat anything your size or
smaller with your mouth; anything bigger eats you the same way. Every meal
fills the growth bar: its thirds are your three sizes, and a full bar clears
the level.

FOUR ZONES, EIGHT STOPS EACH
SHALLOW PORT, ARCHIVE DEPTHS, THE ABYSS, KERNEL SPACE. Six levels, a PING
FLOOD bonus round after the third (30 seconds, eat all you can) and the
ANTIVIRUS at the end. Each stop opens the next. Medals: GOLD with no life
lost, SILVER with one, BRONZE otherwise (the PING FLOOD counts pings).

THE ROSTER
Size 1 eats: PING, BOT, and the rare GOLDEN PACKET (5000, it runs fast).
Size 2 eats: PACKET, COOKIE (not while it is puffed up), WORM.
Size 3 eats: TROJAN, ROOTKIT (half invisible until close), RANSOMWARE
(its padlock lure locks a small kernel in place).
Never: FIREWALL SHARK, SPAM (stings: a stun and a little growth), LOGIC BOMB
(counts down, then blows up everything near it).
Hunters only notice a kernel ahead of them: behind one is the safe place.
The FIELD GUIDE fills in as you meet them.

THE ANTIVIRUS
Bite its tail, never its face. When the warning shows, hide in the kelp on
the sand: its scan beam sweeps the floor. Right after a scan it is UPDATING
and slow, and a bite then counts double.

EVENTS
Frenzy: eat quickly in a row for X2, X3, X4.
Capsules from the surface: S speed, X2 score, SH shield (one hit), EMP
(stuns the hunters, defuses the bombs).
Caches on the sand: +500. The B-Y-T-E letters: all four are an extra life.

CONTROLS
Stick or D-pad: swim.  X: dash (it cools down).  START: pause.
Menus: D-pad, X, O back. Zone map: D-pad moves node by node.

OPTIONS: sound, reset progress. Progress saves.
